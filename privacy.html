Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Price Tag Anatomy website and our in-house publishing tool ("the tool"), which posts videos made by Price Tag Anatomy to Price Tag Anatomy's own social media accounts.

What we collect

The website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it collects no personal information.

When the account owner connects a TikTok account to the tool, TikTok gives the tool an access token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar). The tool uses them only to upload our own videos to that account and to read the status and basic statistics of those videos.

How we store it

Tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in a local configuration file, and are never sold, shared or sent to third parties. We do not collect information about TikTok viewers or other users.

How to remove it

The account owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services). We then delete the stored token. Questions: use the contact details on our YouTube channel page.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
