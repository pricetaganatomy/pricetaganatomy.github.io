Price Tag Anatomy

The money side of American farming.

Price Tag Anatomy is an educational video channel. We take apart the price tags farmers live with: what a combine, a repair, an acre of land or a ton of fertilizer really costs, who gets the money, and what the headline numbers leave out. Every video is built on public sources such as USDA data, court records and company filings.

We publish on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (@pricetaganatom).

Our in-house publishing tool uploads videos that our own team made to our own accounts after a person on the team reviews and approves each one. It does not access anyone else's account.
